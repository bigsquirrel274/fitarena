3. Camera Use

This section covers the app's most sensitive permission. Please read it carefully.

When does the camera turn on?

The camera turns on only when you start a Ghost PvP duel. It never runs in any other part of the app, in the background, or without your knowledge.

What happens to your camera images?

  • The camera feed is processed on your device, in real time. This uses the Google ML Kit pose detection library and happens entirely on your device (offline).
  • Only the positions of your body's joint points are calculated from the image, and your repetition count (for example, how many squats you performed) is derived from those positions.
  • No photo or video is ever recorded. Nothing is saved to your device, sent to our servers, or shared with third parties.
  • The only thing saved to our servers is the numeric result: your repetition count, duration, and duel outcome.
  • The camera session ends the moment you leave the duel screen.

Withdrawing permission

You can revoke camera permission at any time in your device settings. Doing so only disables the camera-based duel feature; workout logging, card collection, streak tracking, and every other part of the app continue to work normally.

Your workout environment

Keep in mind that people and surroundings near you may enter the camera's field of view while you use this feature. Because no image is recorded, this data never reaches us — but we still recommend choosing an appropriate environment for your own privacy.

8. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can permanently delete your account and all of your data from within the app: Profile → Settings → Delete My Account.

For your security, you will be asked to re-authenticate during the deletion process. Once deletion completes, your profile, workout records, card collection, in-app balances, and leaderboard entry are permanently removed.

This action cannot be undone. Deleted data cannot be recovered.

If you cannot access the app, you can send your deletion request to redazzapp@gmail.com.

11. Data Security

Key measures we take to protect your data:

  • All data transfer takes place over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Database access is restricted by server-side security rules; users can only access their own data
  • We never see or store your password; authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication
  • Camera images never leave your device
  • In-game economic values (points, currencies) are subject to server-side limit checks

Please note that no system is 100% secure. We do everything we reasonably can to protect your data, but we cannot guarantee the absolute security of transmission over the internet.
