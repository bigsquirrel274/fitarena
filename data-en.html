Data Usage

Last updated: September 2026

This page gives a summary of what data FitArena collects, whether it is encrypted, and whether it can be deleted. For detailed explanations, see the Privacy Policy.

Summary

Is data encrypted in transit?Yes — all transfer uses HTTPS/TLS
Can you delete your data?Yes — Profile → Settings → Delete My Account
Is data sold?No
Is data shared for advertising or marketing?No
Are camera images recorded?No — processed only on your device

What Other Users Can See

Only the following appear on the leaderboard (seen by users signed in to the app):

  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo (if you uploaded one)
  • Your Rating score
  • Your rank

All of your other information (email, age, weight, gender, bio, workout records, in-app balances) is not visible to other users.

Retention and Deletion

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • You can permanently delete all of your data via Profile → Settings → Delete My Account.
  • Deletion cannot be undone; deleted data cannot be recovered.
  • Error logs are deleted automatically after a limited period under Firebase's own retention policy.
